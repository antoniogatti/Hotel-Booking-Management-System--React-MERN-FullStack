## Address
Via Masaniello, 30 — 72100 Brindisi

## Getting there

### By foot from station
- Brindisi train and bus station is **5 minutes on foot**.
- From the station, continue along Corso Umberto I toward the centre and turn into Via Masaniello.
- Easiest option for guests arriving from Lecce, Bari, or elsewhere in Puglia.

### By air
- Brindisi Airport is **10–15 minutes by car**.
- City bus from airport to town centre: typically **€1.50**.
- For timetables: STP Brindisi — Bus to Airport – Town centre - Costa Morena.
- Late arrivals: arrange transfer before departure.

### By car
- Access from SS379 into the city centre area.
- Via Masaniello is convenient for unloading luggage before moving the car.
