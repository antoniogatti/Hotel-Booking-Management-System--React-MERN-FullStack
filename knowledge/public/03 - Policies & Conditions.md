# Policies & Conditions

**Source:** Official terms and conditions page on palazzopintobnb.com
**Last updated:** March 16, 2026

## Summary
These terms regulate reservations and stays at Palazzo Pinto B&B. By booking or staying, guests agree to the following conditions.

## Booking
- The B&B confirms each booking by sending a reservation code via email.

## Cancellation
- Cancellations must be received at least **7 days before arrival** to receive a refund.
- Cancellations within **7 days of arrival** are charged the full amount paid.
- No refunds are provided for early departures.
- In case of no-show without previous communication, the full amount paid is charged.

## Parking
- Palazzo Pinto does **not** provide private parking on-site.
- Street parking is available and regulated by municipal rules.
- Private parking may be arranged in advance, subject to availability.

## Accessibility
- The B&B does not have wheelchair ramps or a lift.
- Rooms are on the **first floor** and are reached via stairs.

## Check-in and documents
- Check-in is from **2:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.**
- Late check-ins must be requested before arrival day.
- A valid identity document is required for all guests at check-in:
  - passport
  - national ID card
  - driving licence
- If documents are not compliant with legal requirements, access may be denied.

## Local tax
- Low season (January-March): **€1.50 per night**, up to 7 nights.
- High season (April-December): **€2.50 per night**, up to 7 nights.

## Payment
- At check-in, after registration, the full stay amount is due by cash or card.
- An invoice is issued for the payment and stay details.

## Stay rules
- Guests may not alter furniture, equipment, or room devices.
- Toilets must be used correctly; objects should not be thrown into the WC.
- Lights, TV, and air-conditioning should be switched off when leaving the room.
- Cooking in rooms is prohibited, except where a dedicated apartment kitchen is provided.

## Keys
- Guests receive room and access keys.
- Lost keys incur a **€300** charge to replace all relevant locks.

## Breakfast
- Breakfast service is available with room provisions and fresh items served in the morning.
- Additional fresh options may be requested in advance, subject to availability.

## Additional guests
- Unregistered additional guests are not allowed in rooms.
- Visits by friends/family must be communicated in advance, are limited in duration, and may incur additional charges.

## Cleaning
- Daily room cleaning is generally performed between **10:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m.**
- Bed linen and towels are normally replaced every **3 days**.
- Extra daily replacement is available at an additional fee.

## Liability and damages
- Guests are responsible for damages or breakages caused during the stay.
- The B&B is not liable for loss, theft, or damage to personal belongings, vehicles, or possessions.

## Noise, smoking, and pets
- Noise must be minimized, especially during designated quiet hours.
- Smoking is prohibited indoors; evidence of indoor smoking may incur a **€250 cleaning fee**.
- Pets are not allowed on the premises.

## Check-out
- Check-out is at **10:00 a.m.**
- Late check-out may be available upon request with additional fees, depending on departure time and room availability.

## Luggage storage
- Luggage storage is available for a limited period, typically **10 to 12 hours**.
- The B&B is not responsible for loss or damage to stored items.

## Contact
- For booking terms questions: info@palazzopintobnb.com
